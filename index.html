foodsync

A personal food-log delivery tool.

foodsync is a small program that runs on its owner's own computer. It takes the meals its owner has already logged (name, time, calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat and a few micronutrients) and copies each one into the owner's own Google Health account, so that the food log shows up next to their other health data.

Who it is for

One person: its owner. foodsync is not a public service, has no sign-up and no user accounts, and is not offered to anyone else.

What it does with Google Health

  • It adds nutrition entries to the owner's Google Health account, and can correct or remove the entries that it added itself.
  • It reads the owner's body measurements (weight) so that the owner can see their weigh-ins beside their food log.

It asks Google for exactly those two permissions and nothing else.

Where it runs

On the owner's computer. There is no foodsync server and no foodsync database on the internet.

Privacy

The privacy policy says what is read, what is written, where it is kept and how to switch it off.
